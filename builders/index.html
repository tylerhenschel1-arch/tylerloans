AIO Loans Are Not Available for Spec Construction in Texas

The All-In-One (AIO) loan is not eligible for spec construction financing in the state of Texas. Eligible programs are Conventional (Fannie/Freddie), FHA, VA, and USDA — through the Spec Lock (Build & Lock) program. Non-Agency and Non-QM products are also excluded from Spec Lock. Contact Tyler to confirm program eligibility for your specific build scenario before proceeding.

Available Programs

Spec Financing Options for Builders

Every program below is available through CMG's Spec Lock (Build & Lock) structure — letting the builder lock a rate before the buyer is identified. Each loan type serves a different buyer pool, so you can cater to the widest market.

Conventional

Conventional Spec Financing

The most flexible and widely used spec program. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines with competitive pricing and broad buyer eligibility. Ideal for mid-to-upper price points where buyers have solid credit and standard down payments.

✓ Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac eligible
✓ Rate lock during construction
✓ Broadest buyer eligibility
✓ Available for single-family and multi-unit specs
FHA

FHA Spec Financing

Opens your completed spec homes to first-time and lower-credit buyers — significantly expanding your buyer pool, especially in the $250K–$450K price range that dominates the Houston suburbs. Government-backed and buyer-friendly.

✓ Opens inventory to first-time buyer market
✓ Lower down payment requirement for buyers
✓ Competitive for Katy, Cypress, and Sugar Land price points
✓ FHA loan limits apply by county
VA

VA Spec Financing

Houston's military community is massive. VA spec financing lets you tap into a buyer pool with no down payment requirement, motivated buyers, and strong purchasing power. Fort Bend County and Harris County have significant veteran populations.

✓ Zero down payment for eligible veterans
✓ Strong demand in Houston metro market
✓ VA appraisal / MPR standards apply
✓ Competitive financing for buyers
USDA

USDA Spec Financing

For builders developing in eligible rural and semi-rural areas outside Houston's core. USDA financing offers buyers zero down payment, which dramatically increases demand in markets like Waller, Montgomery, and outer Fort Bend County.

✓ Zero down for eligible buyers
✓ Eligible in many outer Houston suburbs
✓ USDA income and property eligibility apply
✓ Strong demand driver in rural corridors
Spec Lock Program

Build it. Lock it.
Sell it. Close it.

CMG's Spec Lock (Build & Lock) program lets builders lock a rate on a spec home while it's still under construction — before a buyer is even identified. That means your end-buyer comes to the table with certainty on their financing.

1

Builder Qualification & Pre-Approval

Tyler reviews your builder status, current projects, and financing needs. We establish which spec programs you qualify for and set up the draw structure for your build.

2

Rate Lock During Construction

Once construction begins, the interest rate is locked through the Build & Lock program — protecting the future buyer from rising rates during the build timeline. Available on Conventional, FHA, VA, and USDA programs.

3

Buyer Comes In Pre-Qualified

Tyler works with the buyer (often without a realtor) to pre-qualify them specifically for your spec home. The buyer's financing is aligned with the locked rate and the home's completed value before contracts are signed.

4

Construction Draws & Inspections

Draw requests are managed efficiently with minimal delays. CMG's builder program is designed to keep your crews paid and your timeline intact — not bogged down in paperwork.

5

Smooth Close at Completion

When the certificate of occupancy is issued and the home is complete, the loan converts to permanent financing and the buyer closes. Your proceeds are released. Clean, fast, repeatable.
