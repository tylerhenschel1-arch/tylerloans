How Short-Term Rental Loans Work

Short-term rental investment loans differ from standard mortgages in one crucial way: they're evaluated using the rental income from your property, not your personal salary. This is called DSCR lending, which stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio.

Here's the concept in simple terms: Lenders calculate your property's gross monthly rental income (based on actual bookings, lease agreements, or projected income from Form 1007/1025 rental schedules) and divide it by your monthly loan payment (principal, interest, taxes, and insurance). If that ratio meets the lender's minimum threshold—often around 1.25 for short-term rental programs—you qualify, even if your personal W-2 income is modest or you're self-employed.

This approach recognizes that a successful STR business generates revenue independent of any single owner's job or salary. Investors across the Houston metro—from Tomball to Memorial, Cypress to Friendswood—have used DSCR financing to acquire second, third, and fourth rental properties that would have been impossible to finance under traditional underwriting.

What You Need to Qualify for Short-Term Rental Investment Loans

While DSCR lending is more flexible than conventional mortgages in some ways, lenders still assess your creditworthiness and the investment's viability. Typical requirements include:

  • Minimum credit score: Usually 680 or higher, though some investors with stronger financials may qualify even with lower scores.
  • Down payment: Typically 20–30%, depending on the lender and loan program.
  • Rental income documentation: Recent tax returns, booking history, lease agreements, or forward-looking rent schedules (Form 1007/1025) that demonstrate the property's income potential.
  • Debt service coverage ratio: Your property's monthly gross rental income divided by your total monthly debt payments (the loan, plus any other outstanding debts). For STR programs, a 1.25 ratio is common.
  • Reserves: Many lenders require cash reserves equal to three to six months of the mortgage payment to show financial stability.

The good news: you don't need to show a high personal income. If you're a successful STR operator in Bellaire, Sugar Land, Conroe, or Galveston, your portfolio's performance is what matters most.
