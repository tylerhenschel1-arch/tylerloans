Top Questions Buyers Should Ask Their Mortgage Lender in Houston

Buying a home in the Greater Houston area—whether you're looking in Tomball, Sugar Land, Cypress, or anywhere across the metro—is one of the biggest financial decisions you'll make. With so much money on the line, knowing what to ask your mortgage lender upfront can save you thousands of dollars and prevent unwelcome surprises down the road.

As a Senior Loan Officer at CMG Home Loans serving the Houston region, Tyler Henschel works with buyers every day who wish they'd asked more questions earlier in the process. In this guide, we'll walk through the essential questions every Houston-area homebuyer should pose to their lender before signing on the dotted line.

1. What Are All the Costs Associated With This Loan?

Many buyers focus only on the interest rate and overlook the full cost picture. Your lender should provide a clear Loan Estimate within three business days of your application. This document outlines:

  • Origination fees and points
  • Title insurance and closing costs
  • Appraisal fees
  • Credit report fees
  • Recording and transfer taxes
  • Homeowners insurance requirements
  • Property taxes and HOA fees (if applicable)

Don't be shy about asking for an itemized breakdown. Understanding exactly what you'll pay helps you compare offers from different lenders objectively. Tyler Henschel emphasizes that transparency on costs is a hallmark of trustworthy lenders—any lender who's vague about fees should raise a red flag.

2. How Does Your Rate Lock Work?

Interest rates fluctuate daily. When you lock a rate, your lender guarantees that rate for a specific period—typically 30, 45, or 60 days. Ask your lender:

  • What is the lock period, and when does it begin?
  • Can I extend the lock if closing is delayed, and what does that cost?
  • What happens if rates drop during my lock period?
  • Are there any float-down options available?

These details matter enormously. In a volatile market, a rate lock provides peace of mind—but only if you understand the terms. Buyers in Pearland, The Woodlands, and across the Houston metro should discuss rate-lock strategy early to avoid last-minute stress.

3. What Loan Programs Do You Offer, and Which Is Right for Me?

Not all mortgages are created equal. Depending on your down payment, credit score, income, and intended use of the property, different loan products may be available:

  • Conventional loans: Typically require 3–20% down and work well for buyers with strong credit and stable income.
  • FHA loans: Allow as little as 3.5% down and can be flexible on credit scores, making them popular for first-time buyers in areas like Spring and Friendswood.
  • VA loans: Available to eligible military service members and veterans with favorable terms and no down payment required.
  • USDA loans: Designed for rural and suburban properties and offer zero-down options for qualifying buyers.
  • Jumbo loans: For high-value properties in upscale Houston neighborhoods like Bellaire and Memorial.

Ask your lender which programs you qualify for and how each would affect your monthly payment, total interest, and timeline to closing. One program may save you tens of thousands over the life of the loan compared to another.

4. What Is My Debt-to-Income Ratio, and How Does It Impact My Approval?

Lenders use your debt-to-income (DTI) ratio to determine how much house you can afford. This ratio compares your monthly debt obligations to your gross monthly income. Ask:

  • What is my current DTI, and what is the maximum allowed for my loan program?
  • Which debts are included in this calculation?
  • If I'm close to the limit, what options do I have to improve my ratio?

Understanding your DTI helps you make realistic decisions about your budget. A lender like Tyler Henschel can walk you through strategies to strengthen your application, such as paying down existing debt before closing or adjusting your target home price.

5. What Happens at Closing, and What Documents Will I Sign?

Closing day involves a mountain of paperwork. Before you arrive at the closing table in your chosen Houston neighborhood—whether that's Conroe, Magnolia, or the Heights—ask your lender to explain:

  • How long the closing process typically takes
  • What the main documents are (Closing Disclosure, promissory note, mortgage deed, etc.)
  • What your exact closing costs will be, and how they've changed since the initial estimate
  • Who will be present and what your role is

Many lenders now allow borrowers to review the Closing Disclosure 3 business days before closing. Take advantage of this. Having questions answered before you sit down at the closing table ensures you're confident and informed about the final terms.

6. What Are the Terms Regarding Prepayment and Early Payoff?

If you want to pay off your mortgage early or make extra principal payments, are there penalties? Most conventional loans allow prepayment without penalty, but it's worth confirming. Ask:

  • Can I pay extra toward principal at any time?
  • Are there prepayment penalties?
  • How often can I make extra payments?

This question is especially relevant if you plan to refinance in a few years or expect a windfall (inheritance, bonus, etc.) that could accelerate payoff.

7. What Are My Options If I Face Financial Hardship?

Life happens. Before you commit to a mortgage, understand what support your lender offers if you struggle to make payments. Ask about:

  • Loan modification options
  • Forbearance programs
  • Loss mitigation assistance
  • Who to contact if you anticipate trouble paying

Knowing these options exist—and how to access them—can be the difference between weathering a tough period and facing foreclosure.

8. How Will Property Taxes and Insurance Be Handled?

In Texas, property taxes and homeowners insurance are typically escrowed (held in an account and paid by your lender on your behalf). Ask:

  • Will my property taxes and insurance be escrowed?
  • How is my escrow amount calculated?
  • Can I see an estimate of these costs as they're included in my monthly payment?
  • What happens if taxes or insurance rates increase during the year?

These costs vary significantly across Houston neighborhoods—from the suburbs like The Woodlands and Cypress to central areas like Bellaire—so getting a precise estimate tailored to your property is important.

9. What Is Your Timeline for Processing and Closing?

Understand the lender's typical timeline from application to closing. Most lenders aim for 30–45 days, but this varies. Ask:

  • What is your average closing time?
  • What could delay the process?
