Construction Loans in Katy TX: What You Need to Know Before You Build

Building your dream home in Katy, Texas is an exciting prospect. But before you break ground, you need to understand construction loans. These specialized mortgages work differently than traditional home loans, and getting one requires careful planning. Whether you're building in the Katy area or the greater Houston market, understanding the ins and outs of construction financing can save you thousands of dollars and countless headaches down the road.

Let's walk through everything you need to know about construction loans in Katy TX, so you can move forward with confidence.

What Exactly Is a Construction Loan?

A construction loan is a short-term borrowing option specifically designed to finance the building of a new home. Unlike traditional mortgages where you borrow a lump sum upfront, construction loans disburse funds in stages as your home is built. These are called "draws," and they're released as you hit specific construction milestones.

This structure protects both you and the lender. You're not paying for the entire house before it exists, and the lender ensures their money is being used appropriately as construction progresses. It's a practical solution for the unique challenges of building in the Katy and Houston markets.

Two-Time Close Construction Loans

CMG's conventional construction program uses a Two-Time Close structure. The first closing covers the construction phase, and a second closing converts the loan into permanent financing once the home is complete. While this involves two separate closing processes, it allows the permanent loan terms to be negotiated closer to completion when the home's value is established.

Stand-alone construction loans — where you secure separate financing entirely outside this program — are another path some borrowers consider, but they involve fully refinancing rather than the structured two-close conversion offered through CMG.

Important for Texas Buyers: CMG's conventional construction program does not permit pre-started or partially completed builds in Texas. Construction must begin after loan closing. If your project has already broken ground or has any work in progress, discuss this with your loan officer before proceeding, as it affects which financing options are available to you.

Key Requirements for Getting a Construction Loan in the Houston Area

Lenders evaluate construction loans differently than traditional mortgages. Here's what they typically require:

Down Payment

Construction loans require a larger down payment than a standard purchase mortgage. For CMG's conventional construction program, the minimum down payment is approximately 10% for a one-unit primary residence, 20% for a two-unit primary residence, and 25% for a second home. Having more equity at the start generally improves your rate and terms.

Good Credit Score

You'll typically need a credit score of 680 or higher, though 700+ is preferred for better terms. This is more important with construction loans since lenders view them as higher risk than traditional mortgages.

Solid Income Documentation

Lenders want proof of steady income to ensure you can handle loan payments during construction. They may ask for recent tax returns, pay stubs, and bank statements. If you're self-employed, building in the Katy area, prepare detailed documentation of your income history.

Detailed Plans and Contracts

You'll need finalized building plans, a construction contract with a licensed builder, and a detailed construction timeline. The lender needs to understand exactly what's being built and how long it will take.

Proof of Land Ownership

You must own the land outright or have it cleared for construction financing. If you're financing the land separately, that adds complexity to the process.

How Construction Loan Draws Work

Understanding the draw process is crucial. Here's how it typically works for Katy TX construction projects:

  • The lender inspects the property at predetermined construction stages
  • The builder requests payment (a "draw") for completed work
  • The inspector verifies the work meets the construction contract specifications
  • The lender disburses funds, typically holding back 5-10% as a cushion
  • This process repeats throughout construction until your home is complete

The final draw is usually released once construction is finished, all inspections are passed, and your home receives a certificate of occupancy. Then your construction loan converts to a permanent mortgage (if you chose the construction-to-permanent option).

Interest Rates and Costs

Construction loan rates in Katy, TX typically run slightly higher than traditional mortgage rates because of the increased risk. However, you're only paying interest on the amount that's been drawn, not the full loan amount. This can save you significant money during the construction phase.

Budget for additional costs including:

  • Application and origination fees
  • Appraisals and inspections
  • Title work and insurance
  • Potentially higher interest rates (0.5-1% above conventional mortgages)

It's worth shopping around with multiple lenders in the Houston and Katy markets. Rates and terms can vary considerably.

Timeline Expectations

Construction loans typically last 12-24 months, depending on your project's scope. During this time, you're managing construction payments while potentially paying temporary interest-only payments. Once construction finishes, your loan converts to a standard 15 or 30-year mortgage.

Planning for the complete timeline—from approval through permanent financing—is essential for a smooth building experience in Katy.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Don't make these costly errors:

  • Underestimating construction costs: Always build in a 10-15% contingency buffer
  • Changing contractors mid-project: Lenders may require the same contractor throughout
  • Making major purchases before closing: Large debt can affect your approval
  • Ignoring the inspection process: Stay involved and ensure quality throughout
  • Not planning for permanent financing early: Don't assume conversion will be automatic
