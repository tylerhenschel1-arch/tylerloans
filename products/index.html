FHA Loans
First-Time Buyers · Low Down Payment

FHA loans are one of the most popular choices for first-time homebuyers in the Houston area. Backed by the Federal Housing Administration, they allow lower credit scores and smaller down payments than conventional loans — making homeownership accessible to more buyers in Katy, Cypress, and Sugar Land.

Down payment as low as 3.5%
Credit scores from 580+
Higher debt-to-income flexibility
Available for primary residences
Seller can contribute to closing costs
15 and 30-year fixed options

VA Loans
Veterans · Active Duty · Surviving Spouses

If you've served, you've earned one of the best mortgage benefits available anywhere. VA loans require zero down payment, no private mortgage insurance, and offer competitive rates. Many Houston-area veterans don't take full advantage of this benefit — let's make sure you do.

Zero down payment required
No private mortgage insurance (PMI)
Competitive interest rates
Available multiple times
Flexible credit requirements
Seller can pay all closing costs

Conventional Loans
Most Popular · Flexible Terms

Conventional loans are the most common mortgage type in the Houston market. For buyers with strong credit and a solid down payment, they often provide the best combination of rate, flexibility, and long-term cost. Options include fixed and adjustable rate structures across various term lengths.

Down payment from 3% (with PMI)
No PMI at 20%+ down
15, 20, and 30-year fixed options
ARM options for shorter time horizons
Investment and second home eligible
Conforming and high-balance options

All-In-One Loan
Business Owners · Investors · Cash Flow Optimizers

The All-In-One Loan fuses your mortgage with a checking account so every dollar of cash you hold reduces your balance — and the interest you pay — every single day. Business owners, self-employed borrowers, and investors with strong cash flow use this to build equity significantly faster than a traditional mortgage.

Daily interest calculation on net balance
Access equity like a HELOC
Accelerate payoff without extra payments
Ideal for variable income earners
HELOC alternative with one product
Available on primary residences in TX
